ABOUT LADY_SMAELL

Who Is Lady_smaell?

Hi, I'm Leah, otherwise known as Lady_smaell. I'm a variety streamer, speedrunner and reviewer with a love of indie games, retro classics and hidden gems. The Steam Clean is my biggest ongoing project, a challenge to work through my Steam backlog one game at a time while sharing reviews, livestreams and discoveries along the way. When I'm not cleaning the backlog you'll usually find me working on speedruns, streaming weird indie games or finding my next favourite gaming obsession.
