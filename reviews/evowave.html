Should You Play In 2026?

You honestly have no excuse to not try this game. IT'S FREE! Evo/Wave is an old school 3D collectahon platformer with an amazing retrowave aesthetic, great music and a banging sense of humour. It's very aware it's silly and cliched but it leans into that and still devlivers an incredibly solid experience.

What I Loved

  • The Vibes
  • Incredible retorwave aesthetics
  • Solid platforming
  • Soundtrack to rock out to

What Didn't Work

  • Controls occasionally a little finicky
  • Areas can be hard to navigate quickly.

Favourite Moment

Entering the first world and just loving the aesthetic.

Favourite Boss / Character

Magnavex. They are honestly hysterical and I love how grumpy he is.

Who Would Enjoy This?

Players who don't ahve a lot of free time and enjoy some great solid 3D platforming. Agin mentioning it's free so absolutely worth a look.
