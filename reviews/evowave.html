EVO/WAVE

⭐ OUTSTANDING 9/10

A 3D collectathon platformer that knows what it is and isn't afraid to let it show.


Quick Facts

  • Completed: 18/07/2025
  • Hours Played Overall: 2
  • Achievement Progress: 16/30
  • Steam Clean Size: QUICK
  • Genre: Platformer

Should You Play In 2026?

You honestly have no excuse to not try this game. IT'S FREE! Evo/Wave is an old school 3D collectathon platformer with an amazing retrowave aesthetic, great music and a banging sense of humour. It's very aware it's silly and cliched but it leans into that and still delivers an incredibly solid experience.

Evo/Wave has a real character and that is shown through primarily its visuals, it takes you through the ever evolving landscape of gaming from the retro wireframe to modern 3D as you restore the EVO/CORE and remove MagnaVox's influence. It's a very straightforward affair but Evo/Wave does it with such sincerity you can't help but be charmed. I will say I loved the neon wireframe level so much even if it did destroy me with it's platforming.

It's all well and good looking good but if there is no substance what is the point? Thankfully Evo/wave has you covered, you leanr a range of movement techniques that mixes up game play and it's always fun to see just how much you can squeeze out of a single jump or glide. The game is also relatively forgiving difficulty wise and I think it has a perfect balance espeically for younger players.

I already mentioned the soundtrack and will just reiterate it is an absolute mood. Synthy-80's electro just dances across your eardrums and lulls you into a retrowave zen state.

What I Loved

  • The Vibes
  • Incredible retorwave aesthetics
  • Solid platforming
  • Soundtrack to rock out to

What Didn't Work

  • Controls occasionally a little finicky
  • Areas can be hard to navigate quickly.

Favourite Moment

Entering the first world and just loving the absolute vibes

Favourite Boss / Character

MagnaVex. They are honestly hysterical and I love how grumpy he is.

Who Would Enjoy This?

Players who don't have a lot of free time and enjoy some great solid 3D platforming. Again mentioning it's free so absolutely worth a look.
