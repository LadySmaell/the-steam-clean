STEAM CLEAN REVIEW

Guacamelee

💎 ESSENTIAL

10/10

Guacamelee is a mexicilicious metroidvania that doesn't take itself too seriously but still packs one hell of a punch.


Quick Facts

  • Completed: 2014
  • Hours Played Overall: At least 300
  • Achievement Progress: 47/60
  • Steam Clean Size: WEEKEND
  • Genre: Metroidvania

Should You Play In 2026?

Seems kind of redundant but absolutely. I LOVE this game, if the hour count doesn't give that away.

This was the first game I actually really tried to get 'good' at and I still love everything about it even after 12 years. The movement is so incredibly satisfying especially once you're able chain everything together, visually the game just pops off the screen. Super Turbo championship Edition in particular has incredible colours and aesthetic. The music absolutely slaps, it emocpasses the mexican hertiage and so easy to listen too. I am a huge fan of the final dungeon track which has a permanant place on my playlist.

I could honestly go on and on about how much I love this series, it has a wonderful sense of humour and while it does go heavy on the memes I personally find it part of it's charm. The game WANTS you to have fun. Despite that goofy exterior there is a true gem underneath. The movement is some of the most satisfying I've experienced in a metroidvania and there are some really challenging areas (looking at you Cueva de la Locura) that will really test your mettle.

And this game loves to test your mettle; the combat is fast paced and requires a good deal situational awareness. The patterns may not be complicated but when you have multiple enemies ganging up on you at once it can get pretty hectic.

Yeah it's fair to say I love Guacamelee in all it's many iterations.

What I Loved

  • The incredible, vibrant visuals
  • The goofy sense of humour and play.
  • Incredible movement
  • Fun and challenging combat

What Didn't Work

  • Might be a bit too memey for sme people
  • Story is a bit cliche

Favourite Moment

The Grand Temple - such an incredibly fun and challenging dungeon.

Favourite Boss / Character

Either FlameFace or Uay Chivo

Who Would Enjoy This?

Anyone who enjoys a good solid mteroidvania, brawler style combat or mexican culture.
