Should You Play In 2026?

Blood Gauntlet is the kind of game that knows exactly what it wants to be. Throwing you into a gothic-inspired gauntlet filled with enemies, hazards and a constantly ticking blood meter, the game wastes absolutely no time getting to the point. The core idea is simple: keep moving, keep fighting and don't stop unless you want your run to end abruptly.

I personally had a great time with it. It's a small game, but it's built around a genuinely fun concept that has been executed well. The controls are responsive, movement feels great and combat delivers satisfying feedback without becoming overly complicated. Before long I found myself falling into that classic arcade loop of "just one more run" as I chased better rankings and cleaner clears.

The blood mechanic is easily the star of the show. Timers naturally add tension to games and Blood Gauntlet uses that pressure brilliantly. The best comparison I can make is feeling permanently stuck in the drowning music sequence from Sonic. There's always a sense of urgency hanging over every encounter, forcing you to make quick decisions and maintain momentum. On the standard difficulty this creates a fantastic balance between challenge and fun.

Difficulty on regular mode felt fair throughout. There is definitely a learning curve as you learn enemy placement, routes and efficient movement, but once everything starts clicking together the game becomes incredibly satisfying. Chasing higher ranks became one of my favourite parts of the experience, and earning an SS Rank felt genuinely rewarding.

Then there's Extreme Mode.

Extreme Mode absolutely brutalised me. I couldn't even make it past the first screen of the first stage. If regular mode feels challenging, Extreme Mode feels like the game looking you dead in the eyes and asking whether you were getting a little too comfortable. While it was far beyond my abilities, I respect that it's there for players looking to truly master the game's systems.

Presentation is another area where Blood Gauntlet shines. The pixel art is gorgeous, the animations are smooth and the gothic atmosphere is consistently appealing from beginning to end. The soundtrack is relatively small, which makes sense given the game's size, but every track fits the action well and helps reinforce the overall tone.

Performance was flawless throughout my playtime. I didn't encounter any technical issues and the game ran smoothly from start to finish. My only real criticism is that it ends far too quickly. The core gameplay loop is strong enough that I was left wanting significantly more content. In many ways this feels like a proof of concept for something larger, and I'd genuinely love to see the idea expanded further in the future.

At its full price of £3.59, I personally think it's just a little light on content. However, Blood Gauntlet goes on sale regularly and that's where I think it becomes an easy recommendation. If you enjoy score chasing, arcade platformers or classic Castlevania-inspired action, there's a lot to appreciate here despite the short runtime.

What I Loved

  • Beautiful pixel art and strong gothic presentation.
  • A genuinely addictive gameplay loop that encourages "just one more run".
  • The blood mechanic creates constant tension without feeling unfair on regular difficulty.
  • Exceptionally smooth controls and responsive gameplay.

What Didn't Work

  • Just too short. The game ends when it feels like it's getting started.
  • Extreme Mode absolutely lives up to its name and may be overwhelming for many players.

Favourite Moment

Finally earning the coveted SS Rank. After learning the routes and improving my execution, getting that rank felt incredibly satisfying.

Favourite Boss / Character

Who Would Enjoy This?

Fans of classic Castlevania games, arcade score chasers and players looking for a quick challenge between larger releases will likely have a great time with Blood Gauntlet. It's also easy to recommend to anyone who enjoys mastering short games through repetition and skill improvement.

Steam Clean Verdict

👍 GOOD

Blood Gauntlet is a small game with a strong foundation. While the lack of content leaves it feeling shorter than I'd like, the core gameplay is excellent, the presentation is fantastic and the blood mechanic adds a unique layer of pressure that keeps every run exciting. I came away hoping the concept gets expanded one day, because there's a lot of potential here.
